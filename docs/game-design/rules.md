# Shatranj: Complete Megagame Rules & Master Execution Document

This document serves as the absolute priority of truth for the event structure, game mechanics, and execution pacing of the Shatranj Megagame.

---

## 1. System Parameters & Entity Overview

### The Event
* **Total Teams:** 10 Teams assigned to 5 asymmetric Duopoly Archetypes (2 teams per archetype).
* **Game Clock:** 240 Minutes (4 Hours).
  * 30m Setup, Briefing, & Faction Allocation.
  * 10 Rounds × 20m per round.
  * 10m Final Scoring & Ceremony.
* **Staffing Panel:** 10-15 Admins.
  * 10 Embedded Table Verifiers: Oversee digital inputs at team tables.
  * 1-2 Central Market Admins: Manage sheet master locks and verify large trades.
  * 1 Game Master / Announcer: Controls timers, runs live auctions, acts as lore herald.

### 3 Core Assets
All metrics are strictly denominated in Tens and Hundreds.
* **Food (F):** Sustenance, survival, and Material engine upkeep.
* **Material (M):** Construction, infrastructure, and Food engine upkeep.
* **Liquid Gold (G):** Primary currency, taxes, fees, and NAV baseline.

## 2. Game Structure & Round-by-Round Flow

Every 20-minute round consists of three phases: Market/Draft, Trading/Action, and Auto-Log. The game is divided into two distinct eras:

### A. The Drafting Era (Rounds 1 - 5)
Standard cards are injected into the economy via tray drafting:
* **Round 1:** 1 Card Draft (3-card tray per team = 30 sent out).
  * *Logistics:* 30 fresh injected. 10 kept by teams, 20 returned. (Remainder pool = 20).
* **Round 2:** 2 Card Draft (6-card tray per team = 60 sent out).
  * *Logistics:* 40 fresh injected + 20 remainder = 60 sent out. 20 kept by teams, 40 returned. (Remainder pool = 40).
* **Round 3:** 1 Card Draft (3-card tray per team = 30 sent out).
  * *Logistics:* 0 fresh injected (take 30 from the 40 remainder, leaving 10 aside). 10 kept by teams, 20 returned. (Remainder pool = 10 aside + 20 returned = 30).
* **Round 4:** 2 Card Draft (6-card tray per team = 60 sent out).
  * *Logistics:* 30 fresh injected + 30 remainder = 60 sent out. 20 kept by teams, 40 returned. (Remainder pool = 40).
* **Round 5:** 1 Card Draft (3-card tray per team = 30 sent out).
  * *Logistics:* 0 fresh injected (take 30 from the 40 remainder, leaving 10 aside). 10 kept by teams, 20 returned. (Remainder pool = 10 aside + 20 returned = 30).

**Summary of Drafting Logistics:**
* **Net Fresh Cards Injected:** Exactly **100 curated Common Cards** (30 in R1 + 40 in R2 + 0 in R3 + 30 in R4 + 0 in R5) out of the 150 available pool.
* **Total Cards Drafted by Teams:** 70 cards (10 + 20 + 10 + 20 + 10).
* **Final Remainder for Round 6 Auction:** Exactly 30 un-drafted Common Cards.

### B. The Industrial Era (Rounds 6 - 10)
* **Logging-Only Rounds:** Standard card drafting from trays permanently ceases.
* Players focus entirely on engine operations, high-stakes sheet trading, executing standing orders, surviving peak winter, and final liquidation.

## 3. Specific Round Events & Taxation Timeline

* **Round 4:** *First Taxation (King's Wrath)* - Wealth is aggressively redistributed to prevent early runaway leaders. The Top 3 highest NAV teams must pay a flat 400 Liquid Gold tax each to the Imperial Citadel. The Bottom 3 teams receive a 400 Liquid Gold stimulus grant each.
* **Round 5:** *Royal Auction* - Elite cards only are auctioned to the highest bidders.
* **Round 6:** *Normal Auction* - The 30 normal cards that survived/were left over from the Drafting Era (Rounds 1-5) are auctioned off. Because logging rounds are light on admin burden, this auction can stretch out over the round as needed.
* **Round 7:** *Second Taxation (King's Wrath)* - Late game wealth redistribution. The Top 3 highest NAV teams must pay a severe flat 800 Liquid Gold tax each to the Citadel. The Bottom 3 teams receive an 800 Liquid Gold stimulus grant each.

*(Note: 6 Calamities drawn from the pool of 20 will also strike during designated milestone rounds).*

## 4. Card Mechanics: Procurement, Building, and Activation

### The 170-Card Database
* **150 Common Cards:** Curated at a 4:4:2 ratio (60 Food [FOOD], 60 Material [MATERIAL], 30 Luxury [LUXURY]).
  * Strict 1:2 Upkeep-to-Yield ratio.
  * Continuous 50% scale variance across build cost, maintenance, and gold value (no artificial tiers).
* **20 Elite Cards:** Multi-attribute costs/yields [FOOD, MATERIAL, LUXURY].
  * Scaled 50-100% higher than common cards, preserving the strict 1:2 total value ratio.
  * Auctioned exclusively at the Royal Auction.

### Card Lifecycle
1. **Procurement:** When a card is drafted or won at auction, it is merely *procured*. It acts as a deed.
2. **Building:** A team can choose to pay the build cost immediately to construct it, or leave it unbuilt to trade or auction off in the future.
3. **Active Deck Selection:** Before a round begins, a team must declare their *Active Card Deck* from their pool of built cards.
   * ONLY active cards will generate yields and consume upkeep/maintenance for that round.
   * Inactive built cards do not consume upkeep, but also do not produce yield, protecting teams from forced starvation if inputs run dry.

## 5. The Winter Upkeep Schedule

Mandatory population upkeep escalates predictably as the game progresses. These values are paid evenly in Food + Material. Missing Winter supplies incurs a severe **Starvation Debt** (-1,500 NAV per 100 units missing, or -150 per 10 units missing).

* **Round 1:** 0 Food + 0 Material (Grace Period for initial building)
* **Round 2:** 100 Food + 100 Material
* **Round 3:** 150 Food + 150 Material
* **Round 4:** 200 Food + 200 Material
* **Round 5:** 300 Food + 300 Material (The Big Freeze Begins)
* **Round 6:** 400 Food + 400 Material
* **Round 7:** 500 Food + 500 Material
* **Round 8:** 650 Food + 650 Material
* **Round 9:** 800 Food + 800 Material
* **Round 10:** 1,000 Food + 1,000 Material (Peak Crisis)

*Note on Market Interaction:* To anchor the economy, Admin provides permanent standing orders: Players can Buy from Admin at 100 Units = 200 Gold. Players can Sell to Admin at 100 Units = 100 Gold.

## 6. Disasters & Calamities (20 Total)

* **10 Continental Disasters:** Affect all 10 teams simultaneously. Includes dynamic stockpile hits (per card owned), production rate debuffs (50% yield cuts), maintenance spikes, and devastating combo crises.
* **10 Regional Disasters:** Flat resource cuts mapped across the geographic belts. Every single town is targeted in AT MOST 2 distinct regional calamities.
* **Universal 50% Stockpile Loss Cap (Safety Net):** Any calamity or event imposing a flat or dynamic resource loss on stockpiles explicitly carries a cap of **50% of a team's total stockpile**. This prevents instant RNG bankruptcy.

---

## 7. Anti-Exploit Protocols

* **Sovereign Immunity Floor:** The bottom 2 teams in NAV cannot be targeted by hostile player actions, and their calamity losses are buffered.
* **Gold Hoarding Counter:** Liquid cash generates zero yield and is actively targeted by the severe King's Wrath taxes in Round 4 and Round 7.
* **Unbuilt Hoarding Risk:** If too many teams hoard unbuilt cards instead of operating engines, global supply will crash, risking room-wide Starvation Debt during late-game Winters.
* **Trading Rule:** Verbal trades do not exist. Only trades recorded on the master digital sheet are recognized.

---

## 8. Master Victory Formula (Final NAV)

At the conclusion of Round 10, final victory is computed automatically by the master sheet:

**NAV = (Base NAV of Active/Built Cards) + Liquid Gold + (1.00 × Raw Food & Material) - (150 × Starvation Debt Tens-Units)**
