# The Geography of the Empire of the Sun

## Terrain Inventory & Rough Placement

*   **Rivers (x3)**
    *   **The Great River:** Cuts straight through the continent from the deep South to the Northern Sea, serving as the primary lifeline and trade artery.
    *   **The Western Tributary:** Diverts off the main river near the center of the map, flowing west to feed the sunken depression of the Oasis.
    *   **The Delta Branches:** A sprawling spiderweb of shallow rivers branching off in the far North, creating a vast fertile marshland.
*   **Mountains (x2)**
    *   **The Eastern Spine:** Runs parallel to the mid-southern river valley. A steep, jagged range that blocks eastward expansion and houses massive quarries.
    *   **The Coastal Range:** A harsh, rocky barrier in the northeast that physically isolates the coastal harbors from the mainland.
*   **Deserts (x2)**
    *   **The Western Sands:** A sprawling, deep ocean of dunes to the west of the Great River. It is plagued by blinding sandstorms and hides ancient ruins.
    *   **The Eastern Wastes:** A harsh, rocky, and arid expanse on the eastern peninsula, stripped of all vegetation and shade.
*   **Seas/Gulfs (x2)**
    *   **The Northern Sea:** The calm maritime border at the top of the map, receiving the waters of the Delta.
    *   **The Eastern Gulf:** A treacherous, turbulent body of water that isolates the eastern mining peninsula from the mainland, forcing dangerous boat crossings.
*   **Plateaus (x1)**
    *   **The Central High Plateau:** An elevated, naturally defensible pillar of bedrock in the dead center of the continent, overlooking the river's main fork.

## The Anatomy of the Continent

The continent is geographically designed to ensure that regions of the same resource archetype are physically separated by vast distances. This forces complex trade routes, geopolitical tension, and logistical nightmares for the players.

At the absolute center of the continent, elevated on the **Central High Plateau** overlooking the river, sits **The Imperial Citadel**. From here, the Sovereign controls the continent's primary chokepoints.

### 1. The Northern Coast & Eastern Gulf
*   **The Delta Basin (Group 2 - Breadbasket):** Located at the far north where the Great River shatters into dozens of streams (The Delta Branches) and empties into the Northern Sea. It is a sprawling, flat expanse of mud and endless grain fields.
*   **The Red Sea Harbors (Group 4 - Wealthy Elite):** Physically separated from the Delta by the jagged **Coastal Range**. It sits on the northeast coast, a wealthy maritime enclave controlling all external naval trade and hoarding immense gold, but completely disconnected from the river's fresh water.
*   **The Sinai Copper Mines (Group 1 - Master Builders):** Located on a brutal, isolated peninsula far to the east, across the treacherous **Eastern Gulf**. It is a logistical nightmare to reach, making its high-yield raw materials incredibly difficult and expensive to transport to the mainland.

### 2. The Central Heartlands & Western Sands
*   **The Memphis Crossroads (Group 3 - Core):** Situated in the dead center of the continent, just south of the Delta and directly below the Imperial Citadel. It acts as the central nerve of land and river trade, controlling the continent's primary bridges.
*   **The Fayum Oasis (Group 3 - Core):** To separate the two "Core" regions, Fayum is located hundreds of miles to the west, sunken into a massive depression in the **Western Sands**. It is a lush, self-contained micro-climate fed by the **Western Tributary**, completely cut off from the main river trade.
*   **The Royal Necropolis (Group 4 - Wealthy Elite):** Located in the deep, barren southwest of the **Western Sands**, far away from the Red Sea Harbors. It is a city of colossal, gold-filled tombs and vaults hidden within perpetual sandstorms. They have immense wealth but absolutely no natural resources or food generation.

### 3. The Southern River Valley & Eastern Ridges
*   **The Theban Floodplains (Group 2 - Breadbasket):** Hundreds of miles south of the Delta Basin, this is a massive stretch of arable land in the mid-southern river valley. While the Delta relies on coastal weather, Thebes relies purely on the seasonal flooding of the Great River.
*   **The Eastern Granite Quarries (Group 1 - Master Builders):** Carved into the towering **Eastern Spine** that runs parallel to the mid-southern river. Unlike the Sinai mines on the distant peninsula, these quarries overlook the river valley, but the sheer cliffs make extracting and transporting the heavy stone down to the water a grueling task.

### 4. The Extreme Frontiers
*   **The Nubian Cataracts (Group 5 - Frontier):** Located at the extreme southeastern edge of the map where the Great River originates. The water here is violent, roaring through jagged rocks and making large ship travel impossible. It is a lush but incredibly dangerous and untamed southern border.
*   **The Elephantine Outpost (Group 5 - Frontier):** To keep the two frontier regions apart, Elephantine is situated far to the southwest. It is a heavily fortified, arid desert garrison guarding the overland mountain passes from unknown territories, surviving on hardy crops and sheer endurance.

### Extra Details for Map Design & Strategic Gameplay
*   **Elevation Dynamics:** Trade flowing North (downriver) moves twice as fast as trade flowing South (upriver). The Imperial Citadel's plateau gives it military oversight over all river traffic passing through the center.
*   **Trade Winds:** Seasonal winds blow harsh sandstorms from the Western Sands across the river, randomly causing delays for the central regions (Memphis and Thebes) while leaving the Delta and Harbors untouched.
*   **The Chokepoints:** The only way to move goods from the Eastern Quarries or the Southern Floodplains to the Delta is by passing directly under the shadow of the Imperial Citadel. The Sovereign can easily choke off the river trade or the desert routes whenever the Room Tax is demanded.
